I have a friend who runs our fantasy motorsport game, it is fun to pit our wits against each other to see who can pick the best squad of competitors, score the most points and perhaps win the competition overall. It is also a fair amount of work to collect the results, award players points and populate the leaderboards. The spreadsheet the drives it all is complex and brittle and not published.

I like to tinker with ideas and I find Laravel to be an excellent platform to try things out and iterate on potential products. The site is pre-production and has a team of people testing the site so while it isn't running genuine competitions it is available to play on should anyone want to at this stage.

This project was started before I was making significant use of AI but it has been relatively easy to retrofit my new processes and tools to an older project to keep adding features and improvements with the help of AI agents.

This is a design for a paper aircraft to print on A4 card and assemble with glue. I was inspired to re-create an interpretation of some high performance paper aircraft I had success with when I was young. The file was produced in [FreeCAD](https://freecadweb.org) and the FreeCAD file as well as an SVG will be produced for anyone who wants to print on good quality modelling card, build and fly!

The flight performance will be highly dependant on the quality and characteristics of the card stock the template gets applied to. The wingspan is such that thinner card will have droppy wings.

I had ideas to use a hobby cutting tool to automate the assembly process somewhat and allow fast iteration on the design however so far only manual assembly has been attempted with some success. A thin layer of laquer has been helpful but perhaps straying from the spirit of using just one sheet and some glue...

The SVG is available [here](/assets/images/plane.svg) and is released publicly without license. While I may revisit this at some time later i am not actively working on this project.
